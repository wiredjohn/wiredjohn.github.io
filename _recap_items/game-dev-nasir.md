Found out about Nasir Gebelli while reading John Romero's book - he was one of the most renowned Apple II game developers who went on to be the sole programmer for Final Fantasy 1, 2, and 3. That's insane!

He's briefly mentioned in this [interview about the Final Fantasy I & II GBA ports](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226150017/http://dengekionline.com/soft/recommend/ff-gba/index.html), which is also a good read.
