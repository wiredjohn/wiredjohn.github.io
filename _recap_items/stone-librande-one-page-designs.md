[Stone Librande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_Librande), the lead designer of Diablo 3, gave a talk at GDC in 2010 about one page design documents. The video of this talk is fantastic and puts a really strong case forward for a less-is-more approach to design problem solving. 

The slides for all his GDC talks are available on his site, [stonetronix.com](https://www.stonetronix.com/).
