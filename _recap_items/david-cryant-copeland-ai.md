I read a few good blog posts by David Bryant Copeland (creator of [brutalist-web.design](https://brutalist-web.design/)) discussing AI as a programmer.

A lot of his feelings resonate with me. A particularly good quote about LLMs that shares my reluctance and awareness of their future:

<blockquote>
&ldquo;They are in such wide use by so many people in the context I must navigate to earn money that I can't just ignore them. To come in as a technology leader and say "AI should never be used" is to make myself unemployable.&rdquo;
</blockquote>

Also the footnotes in his blog posts have anchors back up to the footnote reference - never seen this done before!
