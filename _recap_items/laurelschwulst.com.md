Laurel Schwulst is a designer and educator primarily working with the web and online mediums. Their work is incredibly clean yet somehow feels completely different from any other modern, art-for-artists type websites that I've seen. 

The blog index page of my site was heavily inspired by their [project list page](https://laurelschwulst.com/e/).

The [write-up](https://laurelschwulst.com/e/about-sha/) on their recent web redesign for [stevenholl.com](https://www.stevenholl.com/) is a good read.
