[Studio Octopi](https://octopi.co.uk), who also happen to have a cool website, were responsible for the restoration of Tarlair outdoor pool pavilion. The Art Deco building is stunning and the CAD renders on their studio website look like something from the future. 

This project won a gold conservation award at the [Scottish Design Awards](https://www.scottishdesignawards.com/2025/conservation/tarlair-outdoor-pool-pavilion/).
