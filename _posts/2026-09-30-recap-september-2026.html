    Intro

    This month I've been crunching the Digi-Battle.com website redesign. I really wanted it to be finished this month, but it will 100% be live in October. It's been a lot of work and I've simply struggled to find the time. I'm having a lot of fun though.

    I read John Romero's autobiography Doom Guy and absolutely loved it. I'll be honest, before starting it I found the page count and tiny font size intimidating - but it hooked me right from the start and I didn't want it to end. Romero's life and work is fascinating and inspiring. I felt the same excitement reading it as I did reading Jordan Mechnar's Journals. Definitely recommend.
